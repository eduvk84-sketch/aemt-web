The association

The Masters category, with a voice of its own

Competing, learning and reaching out to the world with the experience that only the years can bring.

The AEMT is a nationwide non-profit association set up under Spain’s Organic Law 1/2002 on the Right of Association. It was founded in Madrid in 2026 to bring together, represent and offer activities to taekwondo practitioners in the Masters and veterans category.

We organise private, unofficial activities: we do not replace the federations or their championships, we complement them with a circuit designed for those who are still on the mat after 30.

  • 01Promote Masters taekwondo: health, competition, camaraderie and personal growth.
  • 02Organise private competitions, trips and training.
  • 03Encourage healthy physical activity from the age of 30 and equal opportunities.
  • 04Work with Masters associations and teams from other countries.

“Taekwondo doesn’t retire: it transforms. Over the years the way you train changes, not the desire to compete, learn and share the mat.”

EL

The founder’s vision

Masters sport, seen from the inside

I have spent many years on the mat as a practitioner, as a coach and as president of a club in Vallecas, Madrid. And I have seen the same thing again and again: athletes over thirty who train more wisely than ever, yet are left without a place. Calendars are built around younger athletes, travelling is expensive when you go alone, and everyone works out on their own what would be far easier together.

Masters sport is not a farewell category. It is where those who never stopped, those who come back after years away and those who start late with a child’s enthusiasm all meet. Each of them brings something valuable: technique, consistency, a cool head in competition and an understanding of sport that only the years can give.

I founded the AEMT to give them a place of their own: a circuit designed for their age, a ranking that recognises their effort, team trips to the major international tournaments and training to perform better and get injured less. And I founded it to bring people together: clubs, federations and Masters associations from other countries. Never against anyone.

I want every practitioner aged thirty, fifty or seventy to know that in Spain there is a common home for Masters taekwondo. By and for the good of our sport.

Eduardo Lozano Moreno

What the AEMT can bring together

  • AthletesFrom every region, age and level, whether they compete or not.
  • Clubs and schoolsThose without enough Masters for a group of their own gain from joining a shared circuit.
  • Coaches and refereesWho understand the veteran practitioner and share methods, criteria and experience.
  • Sponsors and institutionsWho find a single point of contact to support Masters sport.

What the AEMT can boost

  • Tailored competitionCategories, schedules and formats designed around the body and the life of a Master.
  • Health and longevityInjury prevention, adapted fitness training and sport as a lifelong habit.
  • Training and successionPassing the veterans’ experience on to younger athletes and training coaches in Masters coaching.
  • International reachGroup trips and cooperation with Masters teams from other countries.
  • EqualityMen and women equally in every category, with the same opportunities and the same visibility.
  • VisibilityTelling the stories of Masters sport to attract support and new practitioners.
What we do

A circuit tailored to the veteran practitioner

Six areas of work so that experience has a place to be tested, to keep learning and to enjoy the sport.

01

Competition

Tournaments and leagues by age category, with qualified referees and World Taekwondo rules adapted to the Masters category.

02

AEMT Ranking

Our own points ranking in each category, built from the results of the ranking events.

03

International trips

Team trips to international Masters tournaments: joint registration, logistics and coaching support.

04

Training

Seminars and camps with leading masters: technique, poomsae, sparring and fitness for veterans.

05

Health

Practice adapted to every age, injury prevention and physical activity for a long and active life.

06

Community

Practitioners from all over the country sharing the mat, the journey and the meal afterwards. Regional branches as we grow.

AEMT activities are private and unofficial. Their titles and rankings are not federation titles and do not mean official representation of Spain.

Categories

You compete with your own age group

The Masters category starts at 30 and is divided into age bands, for men and women equally. Age is taken on 1 January of the year of the competition.
